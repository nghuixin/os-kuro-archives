# Kuro — health summary, 2021 to Oct 2026

Kuro was a male, neutered mixed-breed dog (Embark: 52% Golden Retriever, 34% Japanese Spitz; the family described him as an Australian Shepherd × golden retriever), born on 10 March 2010. He died on 18 October 2026, aged 16. His regular vet was FurKids (Furkids Wellness Veterinary Centre); he also had acupuncture at Asia Paws and house calls from Petcare. This summary is assembled from in-house IDEXX lab reports, 27 vet invoices, medicine labels, feeding notes, clinic images and WhatsApp messages. It records what the reports and vets said; it is not a diagnosis. Data: [`data/processed/`](../data/processed/).

## Timeline at a glance

- **2021–2022: earlier history (family chat).** Overweight in Jan 2021 (31 kg, up from 25 kg; the vet's ideal was 26 kg in Nov 2021, "All normal", vaccinated). Eye drops from 2021; dandruff and itchy paws from 2021–2022. A leg lump in May 2022 was treated with antibiotics, then removed in a same-day "surgery due to excess fat in the leg" at the Yeoh clinic (Jun 2022). A blood test at a Gasing vet hospital (Jun 2022) was never reported back. A DNA swab (Jul–Sep 2022) reported "high risk of deafness" and "hip problem", which the owner doubted. Raw food until mid-2022, then a PetChef cooked-food subscription; 28 kg in Jun 2022, "very thin" by Oct 2022.
- **2023: skin, ears and eyes.** Seen at an earlier clinic (Taman Megah) in Jan 2023 for a skin infection: skin tablets on alternate days, medicated ear drops, eye-cleansing solution and medicated shampoo. Through 2023 the pet sitter cleaned his ears, gave eye drops and supplements; scabbing and itching came back when the shampoo was reduced (Dec 2023). A "low red blood count" was first mentioned in May 2023, and cataracts in Jun 2023 ("His eyes got catharac"; later confirmed by a vet). First FurKids consultation on 10 Sep 2023, where "his blood tests are all ok" and the allergy cause was unknown.
- **Jan 2024:** a nutritionist's senior-diet proposal cited weight loss, "high SDMA" and "low Retic"; the owner wrote "His kidney not so good" and stopped salty food. No SDMA value from this time is in the records, and every later SDMA (Jul 2024 – Dec 2025) was normal.
- **Jan 2024:** teeth cleaned by his grooming service.
- **Mar 2024:** NRC-balanced senior diet formulated (`diet.csv`): 540 g and 895 kcal a day, 43% of calories from fat; 21.5 kg. From about 25 Mar he was switched to a **tailored raw diet** (salmon head, chicken meaty bones, buffalo spleen and liver, vegetables, oils and supplements). In Apr–May 2024 the groomer found **hair loss at the tail**, and on 29 Jun the owner wondered whether the diet change contributed to his pancreatitis.
- **Jun–Jul 2024: hospital stay.** HCT fell from 34.0% (27 May) to 24.8% (28 Jun) and 13.2% (30 Jun); WBC 26.8–30.2; lipase 5,745 U/L and cPL abnormal (pancreatitis). The vet rated the bloods "critical" and suspected "a mass cancer in the abdomen". ICU from 28 Jun with blood typing (DEA 1+), a transfusion from a German Shepherd donor on 30 Jun, x-rays, ultrasound and IV fluids; red cell count rose 13% → 17% → 20%. A rescan before planned surgery showed the tumour had "spread to kidney and some parts of intestine", an "inoperable mass", so surgery was cancelled in favour of stabilisation and "palliative care"; the family was told he "can live maybe 3 months, 6 months to a year". In the days before admission he had vomited, eaten little, limped and had a bloated stomach. Home on about 5 Jul; fine needle aspiration that day (no result in the records). Diet switched to low fat: Royal Canin and Vet Pro gastrointestinal low-fat food, then home-cooked chicken breast with pumpkin and sweet potato.
- **Jul 2024:** hind-leg wounds from the kennel stay (cone, ointment four times a day; biopsy showed "not a tumor"); house-call vet found "crepitus... indicating athritis"; acupuncture at Asia Paws starts (weekly in Aug 2024; twice weekly in Feb–Apr 2025, Jun 2025, and May–Jul 2026).
- **Aug 2024:** "CPLi back to normal... recovered from pancreatitis"; prednisolone tapered.
- **Oct 2024:** red cell count 26.6 → 30%, liver normal; darbepoetin injected ("another dose").
- **17 Nov 2024:** seen at an outside clinic (Kota Damansara Veterinary Centre) while the FurKids vets were away: HCT 18.9%, HGB 6.7, WBC 28.07, platelets 111. The vet suspected an "immune-mediated issue due to the spleen mass" and gave prednisolone, Clavamox and Yunnan Baiyao.
- **6 Jan 2025:** the vet said the tumour had grown about 2 cm, a "slow increase".
- **2025:** roughly monthly blood tests. Platelets high (586–701) in the first half of the year; ALT above range from May; pancreatitis flare in Nov (lipase 4,451).
- **Apr–May 2025:** ulcerated hind leg wound (Petcare house call with antibiotics and Galliprant, FurKids wound cleaning, x-rays 5 May).
- **Nov–Dec 2025:** SDMA 12 then 8 µg/dL (normal); lipase 4,451 in Nov, back to 987 in Dec. X-ray on 29 Dec measured a 197.5 × 179.7 mm abdominal mass.
- **Jan 2026:** dental cleaning at Asia Paws, from the owner's recollection. No invoice or message records it, and it is not recorded whether he was anaesthetised. His previous professional cleaning was by the groomer in Jan 2024; a cleaning booked for Jul 2024 was postponed because he was too weak, and one offered for Aug 2025 was put on hold. Between cleanings, his teeth were brushed at home only occasionally.
- **2026:** monthly check-ups. "No anemia" in Feb, then mild anemia again; urea high, peaking above 17 in Aug; weight down to 19.4 kg; meals increased to four a day in May; weak hind limbs and urine scald risk; on 10 Jun he was "unable to stand" at acupuncture and "found lying in urine"; on 2 Oct hind-limb and spine muscles were "crampy". Darbepoetin and iron continued.
- **18 Oct 2026:** Kuro died. He was found in the garden fish pond in the early evening; the post-mortem form records "Drowned in the fish pond", but the vet could not confirm whether he collapsed before entering the water or had another terminal event. The family suspected weakness in his hind legs.
- **19 Oct 2026: post mortem.** "Large tumour in the spleen, with evidence of partial splenic rupture" that "did not appear to be a fresh, acute event"; liver grossly normal; kidneys, bladder, intestines and heart without significant abnormalities; lungs showed "pulmonary congestion with possible focal haemorrhage"; some free abdominal blood, "not significant". Stated cause: "sudden death in a geriatric dog with significant underlying splenic disease, potentially involving acute circulatory or cardiopulmonary compromise. The exact cause and terminal mechanism remain undetermined." No official report was issued. Farewell ceremony and cremation on 21 Oct.

## Home care and stools

From the household helper's photo log (`home_care.csv`, `stool_log.csv`). Times are Malaysia time.

- **Heat therapy.** A hot/cold gel pack was applied to his hindquarters, rump and hips in **321 sessions from May 2025 to Apr 2026**: about twice a day (morning and evening) in Jun 2025, then **almost every evening, 7:30–10:30 pm**, through Apr 2026. It began days after the Apr 2025 house call that found "arthritis and some nerve pain". The chats only record warm packs being *recommended* (Oct 2024, Mar 2025, Jul 2025); the photos show it was done.
- **Stools.** 63 stool photos, Jul 2024 – May 2025, scored at full size on the Purina Fecal Scoring Chart (1–7):
  - **Scores:** 1 (×1), 2 (×41, ideal), 3 (×16), not assessable (×5); nothing softer than 3, and no blood or mucus seen.
  - **How they were scored:** two independent raters, who agreed exactly on 69% of photos and were never more than one point apart (weighted kappa 0.61 quadratic, 0.51 linear). The 21 disagreements were settled by a third review.
  - **Over time:** stools were softest in **Jul 2024**, just after his hospital stay, pancreatitis and the switch to a low-fat diet: half that month's scores were 3 (13 of 26), and the mean was 2.5. By **Aug 2024** they had firmed up: 17 of 18 scored 2, the ideal, with a mean of 2.1. From then on they stayed at 2–3, mostly 2. Photos are sparse after Aug 2024 (14 in total), and they stop after May 2025, when the log switches to heat-pack sessions.
  - **Shape:** all stools were segmented logs, pieces or single logs; none looked flattened or ribbon-like. Width couldn't be measured because no photo had a size reference, and 13 stools had been picked up before being photographed, which can deform them.
  - **Dark stools:** several stools had very dark or black pieces (24 Sep 2024; 20–21 Nov 2024). They were formed, which makes digested blood less likely, and he was taking activated charcoal, which blackens stool. But the Nov 2024 dark stools came days after his worst anemia (HCT 18.9% on 17 Nov), so gut bleeding can't be excluded.
- **Animal communicator (non-clinical).** From Jul 2024 to Sep 2026 an animal communicator gave six communication sessions and 43 remote reiki sessions (`communicator_notes.csv`). These are intuitive impressions, not examinations, but they track the clinical picture:
  - **Abdominal pain** after the Jul 2024 transfusion ("up to 8/10"), with a tender belly again in May 2025.
  - **Hips, spine and joints** in nearly every session: stiff walking (Jan 2025), hip discomfort "at times" but better (Aug 2025), and aches worse on cold, rainy nights (Oct 2025 – Mar 2026).
  - **Throat and breathing:** a "stuck" or phlegmy throat (2024–25, when the owner also noticed coughing), and wheezy or "asthma"-like panting from Oct 2025, when the owner noticed more panting at night.
  - **Other recurring areas:** dry eyes, gums and bad breath, itchy ears and skin, kidney and bladder.
  - **Left rib cage and stomach** in Jul 2026; the post-mortem later found the splenic tumour on that side.
  - **How he was described:** "as healthy as he can be for his age" (Aug 2025) and "I'm as healthy as I can be" (Sep 2026).- **Other observations:** a hind-leg skin lesion (Aug 2024, Apr 2025; e-collar from May 2025), a diaper or belly wrap and stained bedding in Feb–Mar 2026.

Treats over 2023–2025 were mostly single-ingredient meat and fish: beef, lamb, fish skins and jerky, salmon-and-egg puree.

The diet review raises two points about feeding: the analysed raw recipe was high in fat for a dog who developed pancreatitis about three months after starting it, and although a multivitamin-mineral powder was added in 2024, the home-cooked lists from Jul 2024 onward show no calcium source or oil, which a balanced home-cooked diet needs.

**Grooming** (`grooming.csv`): home grooming every 2–3 weeks from Jan 2024 to his last session on 13 Oct 2026 (49 sessions), moved indoors after the 2024 hospital stay to avoid falls. The groomer's notes track his skin: tail hair loss (Apr–May 2024), dandruff (Jun 2024), "the skin improve a lot" (Sep 2024), a crusty tail and body odour (Oct 2024), dirty ears (May 2025). Dental cleaning planned for Jul 2024 was postponed because he was too weak.

During the Jul 2024 pet sitting, Cerenia and Yunnan Baiyao were kept on hand "if bleeding in stool or vomiting blood", and carers were told to take great care on stairs "to avoid slipping/falling that he'd hit his abdomen to prevent further injury to his spleen".

- SDMA was billed five times; values exist for Jul 2024 (9), Nov 2025 (12) and Dec 2025 (8), all normal. Oct 2024 and Apr 2026 are described as normal without a number.
- Blood tests were billed but no results are in the records for 8 FurKids visits: 2024-07-05, 2024-08-14, 2024-10-22, 2024-12-12, 2025-07-15, 2025-10-13, 2026-03-16, 2026-04-20 (`visits.csv`, `lab_status`). These need to be requested from FurKids.
