# Kuro — health summary, May 2024 to Oct 2026

Kuro was a male, neutered mixed-breed dog, 13 years old in June 2024. His regular vet was FurKids (Furkids Wellness Veterinary Centre); he also had acupuncture at Asia Paws and house calls from Petcare. This summary is assembled from in-house IDEXX lab reports, 25 invoices, medicine labels, clinic images and WhatsApp messages. It records what the reports and vets said; it is not a diagnosis. Data: [`data/processed/`](../data/processed/).

## Timeline at a glance

- **May–Jul 2024: hospital stay.** HCT fell from 34.0% (27 May) to 24.8% (28 Jun) and 13.2% (30 Jun); WBC 26.8–30.2; lipase 5,745 U/L and cPL abnormal. Admitted to ICU 28 Jun – 1 Jul with blood typing, a blood transfusion, ultrasound and x-rays (INV-8599). Fine needle aspiration on 5 Jul.
- **Jul 2024 – Jun 2025:** roughly monthly blood tests. Platelets high (586–701) through the first half of 2025. ALT rose above range from May 2025.
- **Nov 2024:** vet relayed a suspected "immune-mediated issue due to the spleen mass"; prednisolone from then on.
- **Apr–May 2025:** hind leg wound (Petcare house call, FurKids wound cleaning, x-rays 5 May).
- **Nov–Dec 2025:** SDMA 12 then 8 µg/dL (normal); lipase 4,451 in Nov, back to 987 in Dec. X-ray on 29 Dec measured a 197.5 × 179.7 mm abdominal mass.
- **2026:** monthly check-ups. Persistent mild anemia, high urea, weight down to 19.4 kg; darbepoetin injections and iron from Apr 2026.
- **19 Oct 2026:** post mortem billed (INV-36648). Farewell ceremony 20–21 Oct.
